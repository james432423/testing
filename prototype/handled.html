Being handled

What Ida is doing for each client right now, and what happens next. Clients that need you come first.

How this works: what the words mean

Stuck Ida can't go on until you act. The button on the row unblocks it.

Your answer A client asked something only you can answer. It's also on Today, and it stays yours even if you take over or pause Ida.

Waiting The next move is someone else's, until the date shown.

On track Ida is doing the routine follow-through: replies, reminders and bookings inside your hours.

On hold A responsibility is switched off or paused, so its scheduled messages wait. The row says why and has the switch to resume.

Quiet Nothing is scheduled for that client.

Tap a client to pause Ida, take over, or add an exception. To change what Ida does for every buyer deal, listing or lead, use Playbook and controls on that group.
