# Practice comparison (`practice.html`)

Where the owner judges a workflow in Practice: Ida's private draft next to what the owner actually sent for the same event. Shown for Appraisal check on 17 Ash St.

- Page head asks the real question: "Does Ida's draft sound like you?" The sub-line says nothing was sent.
- Context line: deal, clients, the event and the numbers in play.
- **Practice record** sits in the sheet, above the drafts: "2 of 2 looked right", five slots (ticks for looked right, dashed for this one, outline for still to come), the line "3 more before Ida suggests You approve", and the list of past runs with their verdicts.
- Two columns, same order every time: **Ida's draft** (left, "Practice · not sent") and **What you sent** (right, from the owner's email). Both are `ida-letter`. They stack under 900px, Ida's first.
- Differences are marked plainly: a small numbered ink tag at the spot in both letters, a dotted ink underline on the differing text, and a dashed "No recommendation" placeholder where one side has nothing. No red/green diff colors.
- The `marker` swipe appears only in Ida's draft, on the appraised value it pulled from the appraisal report. The owner's message is never highlighted. One Kalam source note under Ida's draft; the source card follows it.
- **What's different** repeats each number in words (Ida / You), and ends with what matched ("Same in both: …") with a green tick.
- Verdict: **Looks right** and **Not like me** at equal weight on purpose, so the screen doesn't lean on the owner. "Not like me" opens reasons (checkboxes plus an optional note) and "Save my answer".

States: waiting for verdict, reasons open, saved as looked right ("3 of 3 · 2 more"), saved as not like me ("2 of 3 · the count starts over · Ida will recommend one option next time"), each with Undo.

Don't: say "match score" or show percentages, auto-promote, or style the owner's message as the "correct" answer.
