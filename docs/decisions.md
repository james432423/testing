# Ida — brand and product decisions (Oct 11, 2026)

- Working name: **Ida** (Old High German, "work / industrious"). Replaces "Dispatch", which clashes with Anthropic's Claude Dispatch feature. Trademark/domain not yet checked; IDA Pro (disassembler) exists in a different field.
- Design system: https://claude.ai/artifact/NgvypunBzu2oKwC741H6Y5
- Clickable prototype (sample data): https://ida-prototype-theta.vercel.app (Vercel project ida-prototype; production + preview deployments). Also a private Claude copy: https://claude.ai/artifact/FQNzLTMAXCFWwoDjgbwHF3
  - Forest green brand (#1f5a43, deeper #2f7a5a fill in dark mode) on a white sheet over an **oat** desk (#eeeae3); yellow highlighter only on cited facts; wine red only for problems; amber for deadline risks; pale green notebook margin line. Green also means "done".
  - Schibsted Grotesk for UI; Kalam handwriting as accent (circled numbers, one source note per item, never instructions).
  - Wordmark: "ida" with the i's dot as a green hand-drawn tick. This is the interim mark.
  - **App icon: not decided.** Logo A (tick over the i on a green tile) was the reviewers' pick but James rejected it. Six no-tick directions explored; refined shortlist: 2 highlighted i, 5 green dot, 6 folded corner (reviewers split one each; several "i" marks risk reading as an info icon). Waiting on James's pick.
  - Home screen: notebook sheet. Decisions shown as a **card pile** (James's choice over the reviewers' list preference): one card on top with the cited source shown inside it, next item as an "Up next" tab with a quick action; 3–5 items show tabs plus "+N more", more than 5 switches to a list with batch approve; problems and blocked items stay out of the pile, above it. "Worth a look" insight made more prominent; Your work panel (24 of 40, 3-state squares) with a workflow status line; sidebar kept, bottom tabs and wordmark in top bar on phone.
  - Other screens (added to the design system): Tasks (three-column board split by who does it: Ida does it / You approve / Still yours, with "Hand to Ida" links; James picked this over grouping by area), Workflows (When / Ida lines, 3-step trust bar Practice → You approve → On its own, counted track record; James preferred this original over grouped, dots and table alternatives), Your rules (plain-language rules with where each came from, rules suggested from the owner's edits), Connected apps (problems first, what Ida can see and do per app), Onboarding step 2 (typical week as a tick-list map; everything starts in practice mode).
- Reviews: independent fresh-context subagents using GitHub skills (UI UX Pro Max, wondelai ux-heuristics and refactoring-ui, Taste redesign-skill). The new secondary screens have not been reviewed yet.
- Open design items: logo; review of the secondary screens; spacing values to snap to the 4px scale; done rows should link to what was sent.
- Product direction (Oct 11): the sample work is transaction-coordinator style (deadlines read from signed contracts, document chase, inspection to repair request, offer comparison, appraisal check). Basic message tasks overlap with Follow Up Boss/Zillow, so value is deal work. Framing to test: "a transaction coordinator plus assistant for one agent".
- Trust model (James agreed): promotion is per task and suggested by Ida with its record ("You approved 11 of 11 · let it send on its own"), can be partial; rules carve exceptions inside automatic tasks ("ask me if it mentions a price") so routine work runs and complex cases come as quick approve/edit decisions; every automatic workflow has a sent log; Home shows decisions trending down.
- Positioning: "Your back office, handled. You just approve." Chat is a side door.
- First customer: real estate agents, starting with James's mom (agent) and her network.
- Tech direction: model-agnostic via OpenRouter; open models where per-task tests hold up; layered checks; per-task track record as the moat.
- Open business questions: willingness to pay, reliability on real tasks, time as a side project, client-data privacy.
