# Plate

How much of the owner's recurring work is off their plate: one square per task Ida knows about, grouped by area of the business. It replaces the old dot field with something countable.

- `sq--auto` (solid `brand`): Ida does it end to end.
- `sq--approve` (brand at 40%): Ida drafts, the owner approves.
- `sq` (outline in `line-strong`): still the owner's.
- Tasks Ida could take next are not drawn as squares; the quiet link under the key ("See 4 tasks Ida could take") opens them.
- The three states differ in fill strength (solid, 40%, outline), so they still read in grayscale.
- Headline: "24 of 40 tasks off your plate" counts auto plus approve. Never show a percentage of "all your work"; only tasks Ida has mapped.
- Consumer provides: the task list with each task's area and state. Keep areas to four or five, in the owner's words (Leads, Deals, Listings, Admin).
