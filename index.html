The Vision

Where the Atlantic Meets Iconic Design

Rising 24 stories on nearly four acres of prime Pompano Beach oceanfront, W Residences represents the pinnacle of branded luxury living on Florida's Gold Coast.

Developed by the powerhouse partnership of Related Group and BH Group, with interiors by the acclaimed Meyer Davis studio, W Pompano Beach Hotel & Residences delivers an unprecedented lifestyle experience — where world-class hospitality meets the comfort of home.

Every detail has been meticulously curated, from the private elevator entries and 9.5-foot ceilings to the Italian cabinetry and Wolf/Sub-Zero appliance packages. This is more than a residence — it's a statement.
